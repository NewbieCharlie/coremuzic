About Core Muzic

Our mission is to empower independent artists by bridging the gap between their creativity and global audiences.

Digitally Distributing Music Worldwide

Core Muzic is a dynamic company dedicated to digitally distributing music for independent artists. The brand’s mission is to empower musicians by maximizing their career exposure and revenue streams in an increasingly digital marketplace.

At its core, Core Muzic deploys advanced technology to place your music in front of global audiences. The company acts as a bridge between emerging artists and the world’s biggest music platforms by delivering content to over 40 top-tier digital stores and streaming services.

01

Global Distribution

Core Muzic’s network spans more than 40 major digital outlets. This extensive reach guarantees that your tracks aren’t confined to one region—you gain access to global listeners across diverse markets.

02

Maximized Revenue

Focusing on ensuring each release contributes positively to your career, Core Muzic leverages its partnerships with industry-leading platforms to boost visibility and open up multiple revenue streams for artists.

03

Unlimited Uploads

For independent artists building a catalog of singles, EPs, or full albums, Core Muzic offers an unlimited distribution model. Grow your catalog continuously without artificial barriers.

04

Industry Partners

Collaborating with some of the largest and most influential music services worldwide, Core Muzic provides credibility, reliability, and stays on the cutting edge of digital music trends.

Providing free unlimited music distribution worldwide including Content ID and TikTok. Keep 100% of your ownership and expand your career.
