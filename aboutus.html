About Core Muzic

Our mission is to empower independent artists by bridging the gap between their creativity and global audiences.

Digitally Distributing Music Worldwide

At its core, Core Muzic deploys advanced technology to place your music in front of global audiences. The company acts as a bridge between emerging artists and the world’s biggest music platforms by delivering content to over 40 top-tier digital stores and streaming services.

Our Story & Vision

Founded on the belief that every artist deserves a fair shot at success, Core Muzic was built to democratize the music industry. For decades, traditional record labels held the keys to distribution, acting as gatekeepers and dictating who could be heard and who could not. We recognized that the digital era presented an unprecedented opportunity to change this dynamic. By leveraging cutting-edge technology and forging strong direct partnerships with over 40 of the world's most prominent digital service providers, we have dismantled the barriers to entry. Our platform is designed from the ground up to give independent musicians, producers, and labels the exact same distribution power and global reach as major industry players, without ever forcing them to sacrifice their masters or ownership rights.

At Core Muzic, we understand that music is more than just audio files—it's your livelihood, your passion, and your legacy. That is why our infrastructure goes beyond simple upload tools. We provide robust analytics, seamless integration with major platforms like TikTok and Instagram, and advanced content protection through systems like YouTube Content ID. This ensures that no matter where your music is being played or used, you are being credited and compensated correctly. We handle the complex technical logistics of metadata delivery, encoding, and royalty collection so that you can focus entirely on what you do best: creating incredible music.

A Commitment to Transparency

Transparency is the cornerstone of our operations. The modern music industry is notorious for hidden fees, opaque royalty accounting, and confusing contracts. Core Muzic completely rejects this model. We provide our artists with crystal-clear reporting dashboards where you can track your streams, downloads, and revenue in real-time. You will always know exactly where your music is performing best, which demographics are tuning in, and exactly how much you have earned. There are no hidden distribution costs, no sneaky deductions, and absolutely no surprises.

As we continue to grow alongside our incredible community of creators, our core mission remains steadfast: to foster a fair, transparent, and highly lucrative ecosystem for independent music. Whether you are releasing your very first single or managing a massive back catalog, Core Muzic is your dedicated partner in navigating the ever-evolving digital music landscape. Welcome to the future of independent music distribution.
